About the Council
MSRIM
Institute
Innovation
Council

The Institute Innovation Council (IIC) at Ramaiah Institute of Management drives a culture of innovation, entrepreneurship, and startup creation through the RIM Innovation and Incubation Centre (RIIC), established in August 2018.

RIIC enables the creation of entrepreneurs who generate and deliver value. The Centre nurtures ideas, mentors them to develop feasible business proposals, provides funding support, and enables commercialisation of innovations from within the MSRIM community.

A culture of entrepreneurship is developed through courses offered to students in collaboration with the National Entrepreneurship Network (NEN). An entrepreneurship specialisation is also offered to aspiring entrepreneurs within the PGDM programme, equipping them with the knowledge and skills to build and scale ventures.

Startups are provided with physical infrastructure including workspace, Wi-Fi support, library services, meeting rooms, and other essential resources to help them grow from idea to execution.

The Centre is supported by Ramaiah Evolute — the first cross-disciplinary startup ecosystem in India — which aims to harness the synergies of tech-savvy, next-generation, and talented students across the Ramaiah Group of Institutions.

With partnerships spanning NEN, Ramaiah Evolute, WEI, CoCreate, GIGA Innovation, Emerg, AWAKE, and FKCCI, the IIC provides a rich ecosystem of mentors, investors, and collaborators to support every stage of the startup journey.

Support & Services

2018
Established

RIIC was established in August 2018 as a structured incubation and innovation hub within the Ramaiah Group, focused on turning student ideas into viable ventures.

10+
Startups Supported

RIIC has actively supported 10 startups with resources, workspace, mentoring, and connections to investors and the broader entrepreneurial ecosystem.

17+
Startups Mentored

Beyond active incubation, RIIC has provided structured mentoring to 17 startup teams, guiding them through ideation, validation, and early-stage growth.

Physical Infrastructure

Workspace, Wi-Fi, library access, meeting rooms and other essential infrastructure provided to incubated startups at no cost during the incubation period.

Funding Support

Guidance on funding pathways including grants, angel investment, and venture capital, along with connections to investors through the Ramaiah Evolute network.

Ecosystem Partners

NEN · Ramaiah Evolute · WEI · CoCreate · GIGA Innovation · Emerg · AWAKE · FKCCI — a rich partner network supporting startup growth and innovation.
