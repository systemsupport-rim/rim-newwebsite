The Centre for Alumni Relations at Ramaiah Institute of Management is dedicated to fostering lifelong connections between MSRIM and its growing community of graduates — building a strong, engaged alumni network that gives back to students, the institution, and society.

With alumni spread across industries, geographies, and leadership roles globally, the Centre serves as the bridge between the Ramaiah legacy and the future — creating meaningful opportunities for mentorship, career guidance, knowledge exchange, and institutional growth.

The Centre actively engages alumni through reunions, conclaves, mentorship programmes, guest lectures, and collaborative initiatives — ensuring that the MSRIM connection does not end at graduation but strengthens over a lifetime of shared purpose.

Alumni play a vital role in enriching campus life — as guest speakers, mentors, recruiters, donors, and advisors — helping current students benefit from real-world experience, professional networks, and the wisdom of those who have walked the same corridors.

The Centre also maintains the official MSRIM Alumni Portal at alumni.msrim.in, enabling graduates to stay connected, update their profiles, access resources, and engage with the broader alumni community online.

With a vision to build one of India's most active and impactful business school alumni networks, the Centre for Alumni Relations continues to strengthen the bond between MSRIM and every graduate who carries the Ramaiah spirit forward.

Our Programmes

Key Initiatives

Alumni Mentorship Programme

Structured mentorship connecting current PGDM students with experienced alumni across industries — guiding career choices, skills development, and professional growth.

Guest Lectures & Industry Talks

Alumni returning to campus as guest speakers — sharing real-world experience, industry insights, and career lessons with the current student community.

Placement & Referral Support

Alumni helping current students secure internships, live projects, and full-time placements through referrals, company visits, and direct recruitment support.

Alumni Meets & Reunions

Annual alumni meets, batch reunions, and chapter events across cities — bringing graduates together to reconnect, network, and celebrate shared memories.

Distinguished Alumni Awards

Recognising outstanding alumni achievements in business, entrepreneurship, social impact, and leadership through the Distinguished Alumni Award programme.

Global Alumni Network

Connecting MSRIM graduates worldwide through the alumni portal, regional chapters, and international networking events — building a truly global Ramaiah community.
