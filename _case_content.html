About the Centre
MSRIM
Case
Centre

The RIM Case Centre was established in March 2023 with a clear aim to support quality case development and promote case-based teaching across disciplines at Ramaiah Institute of Management.

The Centre has evolved as a dedicated academic hub committed to innovative, experiential, and socially conscious education — bridging real-world management challenges with structured classroom learning through the power of cases.

The Centre provides mentoring for faculty and scholars developing original cases, guiding them through research, structure, narrative writing, and pedagogical note preparation for national and international publication.

Regular workshops and seminars on case writing and teaching methodology help build institutional capacity for case-based pedagogy across all management disciplines.

The RIM Case Centre maintains a formal academic partnership with Emerald Publishing to strengthen academic rigour, peer review quality, and the global reach of cases authored by MSRIM faculty.

With a growing portfolio of published cases and active participation in case competitions and academic forums, the Centre is steadily positioning MSRIM as a recognised contributor to the global management case literature.
