Flagship Programme

PGDM — Two Year Full Time

The two-year full-time Post Graduate Diploma in Management is MSRIM's flagship programme — approved by AICTE, accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), and accorded MBA equivalence by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Structured across six trimesters with 110 credits, it is designed to build future-ready leaders through industry-focused learning.

The programme is also ranked as a "Diamond in Management" by QS I-Gauge, reflecting its commitment to quality, industry relevance and student outcomes.

Doctoral Programme

Fellow Programme in Management

The Fellow Programme in Management (FPM) is a full-time, residential doctoral programme that prepares scholars for distinguished careers in academia and industry through advanced research and rigorous academic training. It offers aspirants from industry and academia the opportunity to conduct high-end research in Management Science.

Duration
4–5 Years (Max 7)
Coursework
12–24 Months
Format

Level
Doctoral (PhD Equivalent)

Eligibility

2-year PG Diploma (60%+) or CA / ICWA / CS + Bachelor's degree
Valid CAT / GATE / GRE / GMAT / UGC-NET score required
Research proposal presentation (10–15 mins) + Personal Interview
Institute Fellowship Support

Full-time FPM scholars are eligible for the Institute Fellowship — providing financial support throughout the research journey. Up to 4 years with 6 hours of weekly administrative contribution.
