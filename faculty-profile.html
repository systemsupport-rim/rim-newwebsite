About

Faculty Profile

Dr. Jeevitha R is an Associate Professor in the Department of Finance & BFSI at Ramaiah Institute of Management (MSRIM), Bengaluru. She also serves as Head of Placements & Corporate Relations, overseeing the strategic placement activities of the institute and managing relationships with 100+ recruiting organisations.

With over 15 years of academic and industry experience, Dr. Jeevitha brings a rich blend of theoretical knowledge and practical insight to her teaching. Her areas of expertise span Financial Management, Banking & Financial Services, Capital Markets, and Corporate Finance.

She has been instrumental in building MSRIM's placement network, facilitating placements with top BFSI, consulting, and IT companies across India and abroad.
