About the Centre

Japan at the heart of MSRIM

MSRIM
Ramaiah
Japan
Centre
Est. MSRIM Bengaluru

The Ramaiah Japan Centre was established with a singular vision — to extend global engagement opportunities for the Ramaiah Group through deep, meaningful collaborative activities with institutions, companies, and cultural organisations in Japan. It broadens employment avenues in industry, research, and higher education for our students and faculty alike.

At the Japan Centre, learners explore the Japanese language from conversational basics to JLPT-certified proficiency, while immersing themselves in Japan's rich artistic traditions — Origami, Calligraphy, Ikebana, Anime, and cuisine. Through strategic partnerships with Japanese universities and Indo-Japanese chambers of commerce, students gain access to international internships, faculty exchanges, and joint research opportunities that give them a decisive edge in today's global job market.

"Leveraging the depth of Japanese management philosophy — Kaizen, Monozukuri, Omotenashi — the centre prepares MSRIM graduates for leadership roles in Indo-Japanese business environments and beyond."

Mission

To build a vibrant India-Japan bridge at MSRIM — nurturing language skills, cultural sensitivity, research collaboration, and global professional networks that empower our community to thrive in an interconnected world.

What We Offer

Six pillars of Japan engagement

01
Language Learning

40-hour Basic Japanese Conversation & Business Culture course; JLPT certification pathways at N5, N4, and N3 levels.

02
Cultural Immersion

Origami, Calligraphy, Ikebana, Anime appreciation, Japanese cuisine, and cross-cultural programmes with Japanese universities & associations.

03
Research Initiatives

Joint research opportunities with JV companies and Japanese firms operating in India and Japan — fostering scholarly exchange.

04
Knowledge Hub

Japanese management & work culture workshops, technical talks, industry best practices, visits to Japanese-origin firms, and expert webinars from Japan.

05
Partnerships & Connects

International internships, immersion programmes, collaborative faculty/student exchanges, cultural exchanges, and active connects with Japanese associations.

06
Incubation Centre

Promoting start-up initiatives with an Indo-Japanese lens — mentoring entrepreneurs who seek to leverage the India-Japan economic corridor.

Centre Leadership

Led by Anjana K.S.

Anjana K.S.
Head — Ramaiah Japan Centre

A Master's in Computer Application and certified Japanese language facilitator (JLPT N2), Anjana lived in Japan for five years and served as Relationship Manager at the Indo-Japanese Chamber of Commerce & Industry (IJCCI), Karnataka for over 12 years. A classical Bharatanatyam dancer and Vice President of BNK (Bangalore Teachers' Association), she brings a unique blend of cultural depth and corporate acumen to lead the Japan Centre at MSRIM.
