Global Mobility

Acknowledging the demand for exposure to foreign culture and education systems, RIM has established partnerships with double and triple crowned schools in Europe, the UK, Asia, and the UAE for students' global mobility. Students have the opportunity to attend classes, network with peers, build lifelong connections, gain alumni status, and access opportunities available to the student community of their partner institute.

RIM has collaborated with triple and double crowned European universities for Study Abroad and Dual Degree Programs. RIM Global Program aims to prepare students for globalized organisations — equipping them with cross-cultural competence, international networks, and a truly global perspective.

What We Offer

Three pathways to global education

01
PGDM with Global Immersion

Credit-based, 7–8 days abroad offering both academic and industrial exposure — a focused international immersion for all PGDM students.

02
One Semester Abroad

Fall Semester at partner schools and universities across Europe, UK, Asia and UAE — live, study, and network in a global environment.

03
Dual Degree

Earn a specialised degree from a partner school alongside your PGDM — a prestigious credential from globally acclaimed institutions.

Key Features

What makes this programme exceptional

International Certification & Dual Degree

Earn recognised certifications and a dual degree from renowned globally accredited schools.

International Mentorship Programme

Six-month, one-on-one mentoring sessions by a global industry leader.

International Internships & Placement

Placement opportunities through the AIESEC Global Talent Program.

Foreign Languages

Proficiency in English and French to prepare students for global professional environments.

Global Memberships

International Student Membership, CISI Membership, and AIESEC Membership included.

Visa & Immigration Support

RIM works with Campus France for visa guidance, scholarships, internship info, and legal support.
