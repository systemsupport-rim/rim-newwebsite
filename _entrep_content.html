The Centre for Entrepreneurship (CFE) was established in 2022 with the objective of leveraging management education expertise to instil an entrepreneurial mindset among students and encourage and nurture student startup ideas.

The Centre serves as a vibrant hub where aspiring entrepreneurs receive the knowledge, mentorship, and practical experience needed to transform innovative ideas into viable business ventures.

Through a combination of specialised courses, competitions, cultural engagements, and incubation support, CFE bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world entrepreneurship — empowering students to think like founders, act like innovators, and build like leaders.

The Centre also facilitates the Prerana Entrepreneurship Club — a student-run forum that organises ideation challenges, market visits, hackathons, and innovation exhibitions, creating a thriving campus ecosystem of entrepreneurial energy.

CFE works in close collaboration with the Ramaiah Innovation and Incubation Centre (RIIC), established in August 2018, which has supported 10 startups and mentored 17 ventures through its extensive partnership network.

Academic Programmes

Courses Supported by CFE

Entrepreneurship Development

Offered in the second semester of the PGDM programme, this course takes learners through the complete venture creation journey — from idea generation and opportunity identification to prototype development and the Business Model Canvas. Students pitch their business ideas to external reviewers, building confidence and competence to translate innovative ideas into actionable ventures.

Innovation & Design Thinking

A specialisation course developing creative problem-solving, human-centred design, and systematic innovation skills essential for building new ventures.

Lean Startup & Customer Discovery

Equips students with lean methodologies and customer validation frameworks to build, measure, and iterate products with minimal waste and maximum market fit.

Venture Planning & Funding

Covers business plan development, financial modelling, investor pitching, and funding strategies including bootstrapping, angel investment, and venture capital.

Project Management

Builds skills in planning, executing, and delivering projects on time and within budget — a critical competency for startup founders and product managers.

Technology for Startups

Introduces non-technical founders to key technology concepts — from digital platforms and automation to AI and data analytics — essential for building tech-enabled ventures.

Club Events & Activities

Talk
Entrepreneurship & Innovation as a Career

Expert sessions exploring entrepreneurship as a career path — covering opportunities, challenges, and the mindset shift from employee to founder.

Session
Why Starting Early is Important

Inspiring sessions on the advantage of early entrepreneurial action, learning from peers who started ventures during or immediately after their academic journey.

Workshop
Ideation — Creative Problem Solving

Structured ideation workshops using design thinking, lateral thinking, and SCAMPER techniques to help students generate and refine breakthrough business ideas.

Field Visit

On-ground market immersion visits aligned with ESG goals, enabling students to identify real-world problems and develop sustainability-driven business solutions.

Competition
Idea Pitch & Hackathon

High-energy pitch competitions and hackathons where student teams present business concepts to industry mentors and investors for real-world evaluation and feedback.

Exhibition

An annual showcase where students present posters and prototypes of their innovative ideas, open to faculty, industry guests, and the broader Ramaiah community.
