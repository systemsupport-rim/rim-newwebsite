The Centre for Corporate Relations (CCR) at Ramaiah Institute of Management is dedicated to building strong, sustainable partnerships with industry leaders that empower students, faculty, and professionals to co-create value through knowledge sharing, innovation, and skill development.

Operating as the Industry Institute Interaction Cell, the Centre serves as the primary bridge between MSRIM and the corporate world — facilitating internships, live projects, expert engagements, and management development programmes that ensure students are industry-ready from day one.

The Centre connects students to a vast network of companies across sectors through structured internship programmes, industry-sponsored live projects, and immersive learning opportunities that go beyond the classroom and into the real world of business.

Through regular industrial visits, summits, conclaves, and academic conferences, the Centre creates meaningful touchpoints between academia and industry — bringing current practices, emerging trends, and professional perspectives directly to the MSRIM campus.

The Centre also supports faculty through sabbaticals, collaborative training, and industry exposure, ensuring that academic delivery is always aligned with evolving corporate expectations and professional standards.

With a vision to make MSRIM graduates the preferred choice for leading organisations, the Centre for Corporate Relations continues to expand its industry network, deepen its partnerships, and create transformative opportunities for every student at MSRIM.
