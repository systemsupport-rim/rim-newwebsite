Quality Accreditation

NBA Accreditation

The National Board of Accreditation (NBA), under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, is the premier accreditation body for technical and management programmes. NBA accreditation is a mark of programme quality, outcome-based education, and continuous improvement — signifying that MSRIM's PGDM programme meets the highest national benchmarks.

MSRIM's PGDM programme has received NBA accreditation valid from 2026 to 2028, reinforcing our commitment to academic excellence and graduate outcomes.

NBA Accredited — PGDM Programme

Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Two Year Full Time)

Valid: 2026–2028 National Board of Accreditation Ministry of Education, Govt. of India

View the official NBA approval letter: NBA Approval Letter .

MBA Equivalence

AIU Recognition

The Association of Indian Universities (AIU) recognition grants PGDM holders MBA equivalence, enabling government job eligibility and further studies. This recognition ensures that graduates of MSRIM's PGDM programme are treated on par with holders of MBA degrees from UGC-recognised universities for the purposes of employment in government, public sector undertakings, and higher academic pursuits.

AIU Recognised — PGDM Programme

PGDM granted equivalence to MBA degree for employment and further studies.

Valid: 2022–2025 Association of Indian Universities

View the official AIU recognition letter: AIU Approval Letter .

National Rankings

NIRF Rankings

The National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), launched by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, ranks higher education institutions across India on parameters including teaching, learning & resources, research & professional practice, graduation outcomes, outreach & inclusivity, and perception.

MSRIM actively participates in the NIRF ranking process each year as part of its commitment to transparency, accountability, and continuous institutional improvement. Our participation reflects confidence in our academic processes, faculty quality, and student outcomes.
