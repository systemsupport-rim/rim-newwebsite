Zero Tolerance

Sanctions for Ragging

Ramaiah Institute of Management maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging. Any act of ragging on campus or in hostels will attract severe disciplinary and legal consequences as mandated by the AICTE and UGC regulations.

Revocation of Scholarship

Immediate withdrawal of any scholarships or financial aid previously granted to the student.

Examination Ban

Prevention from participating in examinations, causing significant impediment to academic progress.

Expulsion

Permanent dismissal and termination of enrolment from the institution with immediate effect.

Filing of FIR

Formal First Information Report filed with the local police, triggering an official criminal investigation.

Criminal Prosecution

Criminal charges and legal proceedings initiated based on the severity of the incident.

Denial of Admission Elsewhere

Restriction from gaining admission to any other educational institution in India.

Immediate Assistance

National Anti-Ragging Helpline

The National Anti-Ragging Helpline is available 24×7. If you or someone you know is a victim of ragging, call 1800-180-5522 (toll-free) or email helpline@antiragging.in. You may also register a complaint at www.antiragging.in.

All complaints received by the MSRIM Anti-Ragging Committee are treated with strict confidentiality. Students, parents and faculty are encouraged to report any incidents without fear of retribution.
