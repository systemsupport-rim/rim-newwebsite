About the Centre
MSRIM
IKS
Centre

The Centre for Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) at Ramaiah Institute of Management is dedicated to rediscovering, preserving, and integrating the timeless wisdom of India with contemporary education and management practices.

Rooted in the rich intellectual traditions of Bharat, the Centre aims to create a deeper understanding of Indian philosophy, ethics, leadership, sustainability, governance, and holistic living through academic initiatives, research, dialogue, and experiential learning.

The Centre serves as a vibrant platform for students, academicians, researchers, industry leaders, and practitioners to explore the relevance of Indian knowledge traditions in addressing modern business, societal, and global challenges. Through lectures, workshops, conclaves, research projects, cultural engagements, and interdisciplinary programs, the Centre promotes value-based leadership and indigenous perspectives in management education.

The Centre actively encourages discussions on Indian epics, ancient sciences, governance models, spiritual wisdom, sustainable living practices, and traditional systems of knowledge, helping learners connect cultural heritage with contemporary professional excellence.

With a vision to foster responsible leadership and holistic development, the Centre for Indian Knowledge Systems seeks to build globally aware individuals who are deeply connected to India's intellectual and cultural foundations.

By blending ancient wisdom with modern relevance, the Centre aspires to emerge as a leading hub for Indian Knowledge Systems, contributing meaningfully to education, research, innovation, and nation-building.

Core Themes

Areas of Focus

Indian Philosophy & Ethics

Exploring Vedantic thought, Dharmic ethics, and philosophical systems that underpin Indian civilisation and leadership traditions.

Value-Based Leadership

Drawing from ancient texts on statecraft, governance, and leadership — from Arthashastra to the Bhagavad Gita — for modern management contexts.

Sustainability & Holistic Living

Ancient Indian practices of ecological balance, sustainable agriculture, and community welfare as precursors to modern sustainability frameworks.

Governance & Administration

Classical Indian models of governance, public administration, and economic thought relevant to contemporary policy and institutional design.

Ancient Sciences & Innovation

Mathematics, astronomy, medicine, metallurgy and other scientific traditions of ancient India and their relevance to contemporary innovation.

Cultural Heritage & Identity

Connecting India's rich cultural legacy — arts, epics, literature, and traditions — with professional identity and global awareness.
