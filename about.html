Who We Are

Ramaiah Institute of Management

M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Management (MSRIM) is a premier business school in Bengaluru, established in 1995 by the Gokula Education Foundation under the enduring vision of Late Dr. M.S. Ramaiah. As a constituent of the Ramaiah Group of Institutions — one of India's most respected educational conglomerates — MSRIM has been nurturing business leaders for over three decades.

The institute offers industry-aligned, global and career-focused management education. Our PGDM programme is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), with MBA equivalence accorded by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Dean's Message

Dr. Geetha M Rajaram
Professor & Dean, MSRIM

"It is a privilege to lead Ramaiah Institute of Management (MSRIM), a constituent of Ramaiah Group of Institutions."

Welcome to M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Management (MSRIM). In a world that demands agile thinking and purposeful leadership, Ramaiah Institute of Management has stood as a trusted centre of management excellence since 1995. Rooted in the enduring vision of our Late Founder and Visionary Leader, Dr. M.S. Ramaiah and the Gokula Education Foundation, we are more than a business school — we are a community committed to building leaders of character and competence.

At RIM, we believe that exceptional managers are shaped not just by knowledge, but by experience, mentorship and the courage to innovate. Our industry-aligned PGDM programmes, distinguished faculty, and vibrant research and entrepreneurial culture create an environment where students are challenged to think deeper, lead bolder, and grow holistically.

Our alumni, thriving across boardrooms, global organisations and start-ups, are the finest testimony to what RIM stands for. Recognised consistently among India's leading B-Schools, we remain steadfast in our mission: to prepare you not just for a career, but for a life of meaningful impact.

Academic Governance

Academic Council

The Academic Council is the principal academic body of MSRIM, responsible for the regulation and promotion of academic activities. It oversees curriculum development, examination policies, academic calendar, and the maintenance of academic standards across all programmes.

Curriculum Design

Review and update of programme curriculum to align with industry requirements and global standards.

Examination Policy

Setting and monitoring evaluation frameworks, grading systems and academic integrity guidelines.

Academic Calendar

Planning and approval of the academic calendar including term schedules, holidays and examination dates.

Quality Standards

Maintaining and enhancing the quality of teaching, learning and assessment across all departments.

The Academic Council meets regularly throughout the academic year to review programme outcomes, address academic matters and implement quality improvement initiatives. For detailed information, visit the Academic Council page →

Quality Assurance

IQAC Team

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at MSRIM was established in accordance with NAAC guidelines to promote a quality culture within the institution. The IQAC functions as a driving force for quality initiatives, ensuring sustained improvement in academic and administrative processes.

Quality Benchmarking

Setting and monitoring quality benchmarks aligned with national and international best practices.

Continuous Improvement

Systematic review of processes to identify improvement areas and implement corrective measures.

Annual Quality Reports

Preparation and submission of Annual Quality Assurance Reports (AQAR) documenting institutional progress.

Stakeholder Feedback

Collection and analysis of feedback from students, faculty, alumni and employers to drive improvements.

For detailed IQAC team composition and initiatives, visit the IQAC page →
