Case Centre

Developing and curating real-world management case studies for classroom and research use.

The Case Centre develops original case studies drawn from Indian and global business contexts, used in PGDM curriculum and submitted to international case repositories. Cases span diverse industries and management domains, bridging theory and real-world practice for impactful classroom learning.

Activities
Case Development Case Teaching Workshops Case Competition Case Repository
Original case studies from Indian and global business contexts
Integration into PGDM curriculum across specialisations
Submission to international case repositories
Business Cluster

BCIC — Business Cluster Incubation Centre

Fostering entrepreneurial thinking and supporting student startups through mentorship, resources and networks.

BCIC supports students and alumni with entrepreneurial aspirations through structured incubation, mentorship from industry leaders and access to funding networks. The centre provides a nurturing ecosystem where ideas transform into viable business ventures.

Activities
Startup Incubation Mentorship Sessions Funding Guidance Demo Days
Structured incubation for student and alumni startups
Access to investor networks and funding opportunities
Mentorship from seasoned industry entrepreneurs
Industry Connect

Industry Institute Interaction Cell

Building bridges between academia and industry through structured engagement programmes.

The IIIC facilitates sustained, meaningful interaction between MSRIM and the industry through guest lectures, MoUs, live projects, industrial visits and collaborative research. The cell ensures students receive continuous exposure to real-world business environments throughout their programme.

Activities
Guest Lectures Industrial Visits MoU Partnerships Live Projects Expert Sessions
Regular industry expert guest lectures and panel sessions
MoU partnerships with leading corporates
Industrial visits and immersive learning experiences
Innovation Hub

RIM Innovation & Incubation Centre

A hub for student-driven innovation, patenting, and technology-led entrepreneurship.

Supported by the Ministry of Education's Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), the RIM Innovation & Incubation Centre encourages students and faculty to think beyond boundaries, develop patents and build innovative products. The centre has been instrumental in generating 10+ patents and fostering a culture of intellectual property creation at MSRIM.

Activities
Patent Filing Support Innovation Challenges IIC Activities Product Development
Support for patent development and IP filing
IIC-aligned innovation programmes and challenges
Technology-led product development workshops
Collaboration with MoE Innovation Cell initiatives

Consultancy Cell

Providing expert management consultancy services to organisations through faculty expertise.

MSRIM's Consultancy Cell connects industry with our faculty's deep domain expertise for research studies, training programmes, market surveys and strategic consulting. Activities
Market Research Training & MDPs Strategic Consulting Survey & Analytics
Market research and consumer behaviour studies
Management Development Programmes (MDPs) for corporates
Strategic advisory and organisational diagnostics
