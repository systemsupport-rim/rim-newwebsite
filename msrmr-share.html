About the Journal

Advancing management scholarship

MSRIM
Management
Review
ISSN 0975-7988

M.S. Ramaiah Management Review (ISSN: 0975-7988) is a peer-reviewed international journal published by M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Management, Bengaluru. The journal provides a platform for academicians, researchers and professionals from business and management fields to publish high-quality, original research.

We prioritise research demonstrating theoretical innovation, empirical rigour, and practical relevance — with particular attention to emerging markets and the Global South. All accepted articles are published with a DOI, ensuring permanent, citable access to scholarly work.
