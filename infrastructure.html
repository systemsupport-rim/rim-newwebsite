Learning Spaces

Smart Classrooms

The institute's infrastructure supports a quality learning environment for students. Classrooms are augmented by necessary IT infrastructure, computers, internet facilities, and audio-visual aids — enabling a rich blended learning model that bridges theory and practice.

Library & Digital Resources

The MSRIM library spans 917 sq. metres with a 300 sq.m reading room accommodating 70 students simultaneously. Automated with LIBSOFT and holding institutional memberships with IIM-Bangalore and DELNET, it offers one of Bengaluru's richest management collections.

Student Residences

Hostel Facilities

The Ramaiah Group provides separate residential accommodations for male and female students, located within the group campus in close proximity to the institute — offering a safe, comfortable, and well-equipped living environment.

Health & Recreation

Sports Facilities

The institute organises sports events providing opportunities for students to develop team-building and leadership skills. An annual sports day for all faculty and staff is conducted every year — fostering a culture of health, competition, and community.

Campus Security

CCTV Surveillance
