About the Centre

Building tomorrow's sustainable leaders

MSRIM
Centre for
Sustainability

Established in 2022, the MSRIM Centre for Sustainability is a dedicated academic initiative committed to integrating sustainable development principles into management education, research and practice.

The Centre advances the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs) by fostering an ecosystem where students, faculty and industry partners co-create solutions to contemporary sustainability challenges — from climate action and ESG governance to urban mobility and green policy.

Through targeted research programmes, ESG consulting, community engagement and policy advocacy, the Centre bridges the gap between academic knowledge and real-world sustainability action, preparing management graduates to lead with environmental responsibility and social consciousness.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Vision
Education for Sustainable Development

To become a premier academic institution in Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) — building capacity, fostering innovations and enabling industry partnerships that drive meaningful, lasting change across society and the environment.

Mission
ESG · Research · Community Action

Equipping professionals with sustainability knowledge, conducting impactful research, promoting ESG principles across sectors, and engaging communities to advance the UN Sustainable Development Goals through education and collaborative action.

Research & Impact

Key Activities

Research & Publications

Published book on "Institutions and Public Policy for India's Sustainable Development" (2024) and multiple peer-reviewed papers on transportation, EVs and urban planning.

Brand Bengaluru Campaign 2023

Led the Agile and Sustainable Mobility theme advocating for greener urban transportation and last-mile connectivity in Bengaluru.

Gender Inclusive Transport Policy

Government of Karnataka funded research project (Rs. 11 Lakhs) developing gender-sensitive transportation policies for urban Bengaluru.

Sustainability Education

SDG-aligned curriculum modules, guest lectures by sustainability practitioners and experiential learning projects integrated into PGDM.
