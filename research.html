About R&D

Research & Development Cell

The R&D Cell at MSRIM drives a vibrant research culture, encouraging faculty and students to engage in meaningful, high-impact research. From Scopus-indexed publications and funded projects to patents and global collaborations, the cell provides infrastructure, mentorship, and resources to advance knowledge creation in management science and allied disciplines.
