How It Works

Placement Process

A structured, transparent and student-first process that connects you with the right opportunities.

01

Registration

Students submit their updated profiles, CVs and academic details to the Placement Cell for compilation and distribution to prospective recruiters.

02

Pre-Placement Training

Comprehensive preparation including aptitude training, group discussion practice, mock interviews, resume workshops and soft skills development.

03

Company Visits & PPTs

Recruiters conduct Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs) on campus, sharing insights about their organisation, roles available and selection criteria.

04

Selection Process

Written tests, case studies, group discussions and personal interviews conducted by the recruiting company's HR and leadership teams.

05

Final Placement & Offer Letter

Selected students receive their offer letters. The placement cell continues supporting students till every student secures a placement.
