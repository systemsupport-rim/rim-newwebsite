Disha is the specialised Consulting and Research arm of Ramaiah Institute of Management, designed to cater to the various needs of corporates, startups, governments, and NGOs through faculty-led expertise and rigorous academic knowledge.

The team comprises esteemed faculty and Ph.D. holders with strong reputations in their domains, guided by core values of integrity, proactivity, innovation, openness to learning, and social concern.

Through Disha, MSRIM offers a comprehensive suite of services spanning market intelligence, business consulting, ESG advisory, and HR consulting — helping organisations navigate complex challenges with evidence-based insights and strategic rigour.

The Centre also encompasses the Ramaiah Innovation and Incubation Centre (RIIC), which supports startups through mentorship, physical infrastructure, funding support, and a robust ecosystem of partners including Ramaiah Evolute — the first cross-disciplinary startup ecosystem in India.

A culture of entrepreneurship is developed through courses offered to students in collaboration with the National Entrepreneurship Network, with an entrepreneurship specialisation available to aspiring founders within the PGDM programme.

Together, Disha and the RIIC position MSRIM as a vibrant hub for applied research, business consulting, innovation, and entrepreneurial development — creating value for organisations, students, and society at large.
